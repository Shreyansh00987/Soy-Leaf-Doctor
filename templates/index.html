Split-Conformal Prediction
90% Statistical Coverage

Standard AI models output single overconfident probabilities even when confusing lookalike diseases (such as Asian Rust vs. Septoria Brown Spot). HARITVA implements non-parametric Split-Conformal calibration (α=0.10, q̂=0.8683). When visual ambiguity is detected, the model outputs a differential candidate set with dual-action advisory, or abstains gracefully.

Pre-Inference Optical Quality Gate

Outdoor field photographs often suffer from severe motion blur, camera defocus, and harsh noon glare. HARITVA implements a zero-latency physics-based filter before neural scoring:

  • Laplacian Variance Blur Check: Rejects camera shake < 80.0 to prevent hallucinations.
  • Luminance Histogram Exposure: Flags severe shadows (< 40) or bleaching (> 215).
  • Interactive Native Re-take Guidance: Alerts farmers in Hindi/Marathi to retake sharp photos.

Field Video Canopy Scan

Instead of inspecting leaves one-by-one, field workers can capture a continuous 5-second video walking down crop rows. HARITVA samples temporal keyframes, applies entropy filtering to discard blurred frames, and aggregates a plot-level canopy health index.

Explainable AI with Grad-CAM Lesion Heatmaps

Deep neural networks are typically black boxes. HARITVA extracts gradients from MobileNetV3's final depthwise convolutional bottleneck to construct high-resolution spatial heatmaps, proving to farmers and scientists that decisions are driven by fungal pustules, not soil or leaf margins.
